Attribute as: "Arnona Israel — https://github.com/mikey641/arnona-israel".

The underlying rates are public: each row is taken from a local authority's official annual
Arnona order (צו ארנונה), linked in that row's `source_url`. The official order is the
legally binding source. Extraction is automated and may contain errors; rows that failed an
automatic sanity check carry `needs_review: true`.
